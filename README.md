# Eterna Clarity

Eterna Clarity is a small systems company in Calgary, Canada. We build practical AI systems for small businesses: Xyterna, Business Health and Custom Systems.

**Complexity in. Clarity out.**

## What we build

- **Xyterna**: take a photo or upload a file, and move on. Xyterna processes it, organizes it, remembers it and connects related information over time. [eternaclarity.com/xyterna](https://eternaclarity.com/xyterna)
- **Business Health**: a structured look at what is working in a business, what is weak and what was never built. [eternaclarity.com/businesshealth](https://eternaclarity.com/businesshealth)
- **Custom Systems**: the missing layer around what already works, so leads, follow-ups, approvals and status checks stop routing through the owner. [eternaclarity.com/customsystems](https://eternaclarity.com/customsystems)

## How Eterna works

Eterna runs on its own systems. Founder Jesse Gamble directs and operates the work; the systems are built and maintained with AI assistance rather than written by hand. We write about the decisions in our [Editorials](https://eternaclarity.com/editorials/) and post short videos on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@EternaClarity) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/eternaclarity).
